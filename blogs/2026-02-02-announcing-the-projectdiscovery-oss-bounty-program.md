Update: OSS Bounty Program Closed The ProjectDiscovery OSS Bounty Program is currently closed. We launched the program to reward meaningful open-source contributions, but over time we saw a growing volume of low-quality and heavily AI-generated submissions that created more review overhead than value for maintainers. For now, we’ve decided to shut down the program.
